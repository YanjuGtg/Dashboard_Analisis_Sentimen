GBFS Emergency Route Finder

Undergraduate thesis · 2026 – Present

Research on the effectiveness of the Greedy Best First Search algorithm for finding routes to Emergency Installations (IGD) in Kota Medan.

  • Tested routes from 5 origin points across Medan to 56 active IGDs from SIRS Online Kemenkes RI.
  • Modular Python app with heapq-based GBFS, Haversine heuristic and an OSMnx-compatible road graph.
  • Folium interactive maps with animated routes, plus a Tkinter GUI.
  • Evaluated computation time, route length and nodes explored, with CSV export of results and step-by-step logs.
  • Ranks all 56 IGDs from nearest to farthest, extended with traffic light and traffic density metrics.
